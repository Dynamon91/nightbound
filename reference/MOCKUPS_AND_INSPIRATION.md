## Visual goals for Nightbound

- Third-person camera and a readable fantasy-bat silhouette.
- Broad interconnected spaces with several routes, vertical layers, glide gaps and optional nooks.
- Distinct landmarks visible from a distance to help players navigate.
- Cool moonlight contrasted with warm lanterns and violet magical accents.
- Collectibles placed along main paths, risky detours and hidden routes.
- Secrets and ability-gated paths that reward returning to previous areas.
- Eight planned realms with distinct themes; this is a design target, not a statement that eight realms are already implemented.

## Asset provenance checklist

2. Type: generated mockup, actual in-game screenshot, or third-party reference.
3. Creator/source URL.
4. License or permission status.
5. Intended use in development.

Never describe concept art as an actual game screenshot.
